Tips
  • Dark room, screen brightness at max, phone held upright, wave side to side.
  • OLED screens work far better than LCD (true black, no glowing rectangle).
  • Smooth, wide, steady swings give the sharpest text. Horizontal sharpness is capped by refresh rate × swing time, so fewer letters or slower, wider swings help.
  • If the left-to-right and right-to-left swings show two offset copies, adjust Timing trim until they line up.
  • Double-tap the screen (or use Back) to exit.
